# Acceptable use

SentinelMDM is a device-management and support platform. It is powerful by design, and
it must only be used lawfully and transparently.

## Permitted

- Managing devices you own or are responsible for (personal, family, or company-owned).
- Parental monitoring of a child's device where you have legal authority and the child
  is informed in an age-appropriate way.
- IT support of devices with the user's informed consent, under a documented support
  agreement.
- Security research on your own devices and infrastructure.

## Prohibited

- Covert monitoring of another adult's device.
- Stalking, harassment, domestic abuse, or coercive control.
- Monitoring employees or other people without a lawful basis and clear notice.
- Tracking a person without their informed consent.
- Any use that violates wiretap, privacy, computer-misuse, or child-protection law in
  your jurisdiction.
- Deploying the software as malware, a "rat", or a stalkerware product.
- Circumventing a device owner's or platform's security controls without authorization.

## Enforcement

The maintainers do not support prohibited uses. Contributions that make covert
deployment easier, hide the agent from the device user, or bypass consent will be
rejected. Reports of misuse may be referred to the relevant platform or authorities.

This document is a statement of intent, not legal advice. You are responsible for
ensuring that your deployment complies with the law.
